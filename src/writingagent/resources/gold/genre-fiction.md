# Gold exemplar — genre fiction register (match momentum, hook, and dialogue with stakes)

The elevator stopped between floors, and Dolan knew before the lights died that they had
found him. He had ninety seconds, maybe less, and a maintenance hatch he was eleven years
too old to fit through cleanly. He went for it anyway.

"You're early," he said to the camera in the corner, because if they were listening it
would buy a breath, and a breath was a currency he was short on. The hatch gave with a
shriek of paint. Cold air and the smell of grease. Somewhere above him a door rolled open
and a voice he had hoped never to hear again said his name like it was already on a
headstone.

He climbed. The cable hummed under his palm, taut as a held note, and he thought: if it
moves, I'm a smear on the seventh floor. Then the cable moved.
