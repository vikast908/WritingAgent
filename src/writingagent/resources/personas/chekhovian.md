# Persona exemplar — Chekhovian (match the manner, not the content)

After the funeral the brother stayed an extra day to settle the house. There was less to settle than
he'd feared. In the kitchen drawer he found a ball of string too short to be useful and too long, his
mother had evidently decided, to throw away; she had wound it carefully all the same. He stood with it
in his palm for a while. From the yard came the sound of the neighbor beating a rug, the same dull
rhythm she beat it to every Thursday, indifferent and exact. He put the string back in the drawer and
closed it. On the train home he meant to think about his mother and found, instead, that he was
thinking about whether he had locked the door, and this troubled him more than the funeral had, though
he could not have said why, and did not try.
