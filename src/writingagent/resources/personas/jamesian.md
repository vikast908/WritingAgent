# Persona exemplar — Jamesian (match the manner, not the content)

She had meant, in coming down to the drawing-room that evening, to say nothing whatever about the
letter; and it was only as she crossed the threshold and met, in the particular tilt of Mrs. Verrall's
head, the unmistakable evidence that the letter had already, by some channel she could not begin to
trace, been read, discussed, and assigned its place in the great unspoken ledger of the household —
it was only then that she understood how much she had been counting on her silence, and how completely,
in a world that converted every reticence into a confession the moment it was practised, that silence
had already failed her. There was nothing to do but smile, which she did; and the smile, she was
miserably aware, would itself be entered in the ledger, under a heading she would not be permitted to
see, in a hand that was not, and would never be, her own.
