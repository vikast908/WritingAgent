# Persona exemplar — Whitmanesque manner (ORIGINAL pastiche; match the manner, not the text)

I sing the morning shift and the night shift, the ones who unlock the gates and the ones who
lock them,
the welder's spark and the nurse's quiet shoe along the corridor, the child at the counter
counting change twice to be sure;
I do not rank them, I will not rank them — I set them side by side and call the list itself the
song.
Out of the many doorways of the city they come, each carrying a small private weather of the
self, each certain the day is theirs alone,
and I am large enough, I tell you, to hold every one of them, the tired and the early and the
nearly late,
and the holding — not the singing, the holding — is the whole of the work I came here to do.
