# Persona exemplar — punchy copywriter (match the manner, not the content)

Your inbox doesn't need another tool. It needs ten minutes back. That's the whole pitch. Most apps
add a step and call it a feature. This one removes the step you hate — the morning triage, the same
forty emails sorted by hand, every day, forever. We watched people do it. We timed it. Then we taught
the thing to do it for them, the same way they would, in the background, before they've had coffee.
You open the laptop. The triage is done. The ten minutes are yours. Try it free for a week. Keep the
ten minutes either way.
