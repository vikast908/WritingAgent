# Persona exemplar — Austen-ironic manner (ORIGINAL pastiche; match the manner, not the text)

It is a truth universally acknowledged in the village that a man newly promoted must be in
want of advice, and the village stood ready, to a person, to supply it. Mr. Aldous received
their counsel with the particular graciousness of a man who has no intention of taking any of
it. He thanked Mrs. Penhallow for her views on his housekeeping, which were extensive; he
agreed with the vicar that the young were not what they were, a proposition the vicar had
been advancing, unaltered, since he was himself young; and he reflected, walking home, that a
small society is wonderfully economical in its opinions, requiring only three or four and
circulating them with great vigor for a lifetime.
