# Persona exemplar — Miltonic (match the manner, not the content)

Not in the sudden ruin, nor the loud
And public toppling of the gilded throne,
But in the small unwitnessed hour, when first
The mind, long sovereign over its own dark,
Consents — half-knowing, and the half it knows
It will not look upon — to call the worse
The better, and to name necessity
The thing it merely wished: there falls the fall,
Soundless, without a watching star to mark
The instant of the turning, or to weep.
For ruin asks no thunder. It is done
In rooms, in silences, by reasonable men
Who, having weighed the lesser against the less,
Rise satisfied, and do not feel the floor
Already sloping gently toward the deep.
