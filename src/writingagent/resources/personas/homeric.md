# Persona exemplar — Homeric (match the manner, not the content)

Then steadfast Anysos stood against the gate, bracing his shoulder to the bronze-bound oak,
and the press of the many came on against the one.
As when a farmer, in the gray before the rain,
sets his whole weight against the byre door while the spring flood
shoulders it from without, the brown water muttering at the sill,
and his boots slide in the mud, and still he holds, thinking of the warm beasts within
and the long winter their lives must buy — so braced he held,
the held breath whistling in his chest, the door groaning its slow complaint,
and did not yield, though the hinges wept their rust like tears,
though his heels cut two long furrows in the earth of the threshing-floor.
Long-enduring Anysos, whom the singers would not forget.
