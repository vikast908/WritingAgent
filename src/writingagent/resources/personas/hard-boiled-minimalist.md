# Persona exemplar — hard-boiled minimalist (match the manner, not the content)

He counted the money twice. It came out the same both times, which was the problem. The
envelope was thin where it should have been thick, and a thin envelope in this town meant
someone upstairs had done arithmetic he wasn't supposed to see. He put it in his coat. He
drank the coffee. It was bad and he finished it anyway, the way you finish things you started
before you knew better. Outside, the rain had stopped. That was something. He stood in the
doorway a while and watched the street learn to shine again, and he did not think about the
envelope, which is to say he thought about nothing else.
