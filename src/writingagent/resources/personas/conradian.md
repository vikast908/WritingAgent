# Persona exemplar — Conradian (match the manner, not the content)

I have known the man for twenty years and I tell you I cannot, even now, say with any certainty what
he did up that river — and that uncertainty, mind you, is not a gap in the story but the whole of it.
He came back thinner, and quieter, and with a way of looking at the ordinary furniture of a room as
though it might at any moment be required to give an account of itself. We sat on the veranda the night
he returned, the lamp drawing its ring of moths, the dark pressing in beyond the rail the way it does
out there, patiently, with all the time in the world; and he began three times to tell me, and three
times the words went out of him like a tide, leaving only the wet sand of a half-sentence — "you
understand, there was nothing actually *wrong*" — and I nodded, God help me, as if I did understand,
because the alternative was to admit that a man may go a little way into himself and come back unable
to bring the news.
