# Persona exemplar — Wildean manner (ORIGINAL pastiche; match the manner, not the text)

The trouble with good advice is that it is invariably given by people who have had no
occasion to take it. My uncle, who had failed at everything a gentleman is permitted to fail
at and one or two things he is not, considered himself uniquely qualified to counsel the
young, and did so at every dinner, where escape was impossible and the wine made his wisdom
seem, for a sentence or two, almost endurable. He held that experience was merely the name we
give to our mistakes; he had accordingly amassed a great deal of experience, and was generous
with all of it. To be talked about, he often said, was dreadful, but to be talked about
correctly was worse, for it left one nothing to deny.
