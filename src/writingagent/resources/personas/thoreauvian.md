# Persona exemplar — Thoreauvian (match the manner, not the content)

I have watched the same spider for eleven mornings rebuild the same web across the same gap in the rail
fence, and I begin to think she is the only honest economist I know. She does not build against a flood
of flies that may never come; she builds for the flies of this morning, this gap, this light, and when
the wind takes it down by noon she does not write to the newspapers about the wind. She simply has the
afternoon free. We, meanwhile, have invented a hundred labors to spare ourselves the one labor of
deciding what we are for, and we call the hundred industry and the one idleness. I would not be quick
to say which of us, the spider or the man hurrying past her to the depot, has the better grasp of
profit and loss. She spends her whole estate each day and goes to sleep solvent. He saves against a
winter he has filled, by his saving, with the cold he meant to escape.
