# Persona exemplar — Gogolian (match the manner, not the content)

It must be said at once that Porfiry Stepanovich owned a hat of such surpassing ordinariness that it
had become, in the small world of the registry office, a kind of monument. No one could have described
it — was it gray? was it brown? it was the color of a Tuesday afternoon — and yet every clerk on the
third floor would have known it instantly among ten thousand hats, would have staked his pension on it,
would have wept to see it wronged. So that when, on the morning of the audit, the hat was discovered to
have been sat upon — flattened, ruined, reduced to a sort of geological event in felt — the office did
not, as you might expect, laugh. No. A hush fell. Strong men looked at their ledgers. And Porfiry
Stepanovich, lifting the wreck of it in two hands as one lifts a drowned thing from a pond, said only,
in a voice gone strange and far away, "But I had not yet finished wearing it," — and somehow this was
the saddest sentence any of them had ever heard, and not one of them could have told you why.
