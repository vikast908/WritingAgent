# Gold exemplar — screenplay register (match format, present-tense action, subtext)

INT. DINER — NIGHT

Rain on the window. The place is empty except for ELI (40s, tired in a way sleep won't fix)
and a WAITRESS refilling sugar jars she has refilled a thousand times.

The bell over the door rings. NINA (30s, soaked, a duffel bag she holds like it might run)
slides into the booth across from Eli without being asked.

NINA
You changed your number.

ELI
I changed a lot of things.

He turns his coffee cup a quarter turn. Doesn't drink it.

NINA
I'm not here for money.

ELI
You always say that first. That's how I know.

The waitress sets down a second cup without asking. Nina wraps both hands around it. Outside,
headlights sweep the lot and slow. Eli watches them in the reflection, not the window.

ELI (CONT'D)
How long do we have before whoever drove you here gets tired of waiting?
