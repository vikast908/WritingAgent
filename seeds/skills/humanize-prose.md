## When to apply
All prose, as a craft default. Apply to every paragraph before committing.

## Technique
- **No em-dashes or en-dashes.** Rewrite with a comma, semicolon, period, or parentheses.
- **Remove inflated significance:** "pivotal moment", "transformative", "groundbreaking",
  "serves as a testament", "left an indelible mark", "unwavering commitment", "stark reminder".
- **Remove symbolic language:** "reflecting", "showcasing", "symbolizing", "a tapestry of",
  "a symphony of", "a beacon of hope", "deeply rooted".
- **Replace weak construction verbs:** "serves as" → is; "features" → has; "boasts" → has/offers.
  Use the most direct verb available.
- **Remove vague expert attributions.** Name the specific source or rewrite without the attribution.
- **No synonym cycling.** Repeat the clearest term rather than hunting synonyms for variety.
- **Remove filler openers:** "In today's world", "It's important to note", "When it comes to",
  "At the end of the day", "In the realm of", "Look no further", "Let me know if this helps!".
- **Remove AI transition phrases:** furthermore, moreover, "that being said", "in essence",
  "it is worth noting that", "to put it simply", "at its core".
- **Vary sentence length.** Short sentences are powerful. Allow fragments for emphasis.
  Avoid three consecutive sentences of similar length.
- **Cut hedging and filler words:** really, very, quite, basically, essentially, certainly, absolutely.
- **No rule-of-three overuse.** One structured three-part list per 500 words maximum.

## Anti-pattern it replaces
Uniform sentence rhythm, em-dash overuse, inflated language, vague attributions,
and generic phrasing that signals machine authorship. Human writing is intentional,
specific, and pattern-breaking — not statistically average.
