## When to apply
When adding a public alert system (alarms, tones, broadcasts) to signal plot events, first test whether it aligns with the world's secrecy and control mechanisms.

## Technique
- Ask: Would the Bureau want citizens to know about this anomaly? If not, replace the public alarm with a silent, internal escalation (e.g., a code flashed on a wristband, a discreet messenger) or a false-cover story (e.g., 'fire drill').
- If the alarm is used, show citizens reacting with practiced indifference or fear, and justify why the Bureau risks public knowledge.
- Use the world rule about 'social heresy' to guide the tone of any public announcement—make it sound routine, never alarming.

## Anti-pattern it replaces
Adding a loud, distinctive alarm for a misprint that would shatter public trust or invite questions, contradicting the world rule that the Bureau operates quietly to maintain the illusion of certainty.
