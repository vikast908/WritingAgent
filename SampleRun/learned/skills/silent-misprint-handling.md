## When to apply
When depicting Bureau response to a misprint or anomaly, especially in a public or semi-public space.

## Technique
- Use a chain of quiet signals: a specific glance, a code on a handheld device, a whispered call over comms, a change in body language.
- If a physical encounter is required (e.g., detaining Lena), have it done by plainclothes personnel who blend in (e.g., a 'maintenance worker' or 'supervisor').
- Never use loudspeakers, alarms, or visible force that would attract civilian attention; the Bureau's power lies in invisibility and normalcy.

## Anti-pattern it replaces
Using a 'Misprint Alert' tone or a visible security sweep that would alert bystanders to a system failure, violating the world rule that the Bureau must maintain public trust in its infallibility.
