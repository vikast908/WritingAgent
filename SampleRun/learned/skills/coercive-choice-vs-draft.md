---
name: Coercive Choice vs. Draft
genre_tags: [dystopian noir thriller]
status: candidate
---

## When to apply
When the blueprint specifies a protagonist is 'drafted' or coerced into a role, avoid giving her a meaningful choice that dilutes the noir dread. Apply in scenes where the protagonist confronts the system's power.

## Technique
- Replace an offer with an ultimatum or an order that leaves no real alternative.
- Use bureaucratic language that frames the choice as a foregone conclusion (e.g., 'You will report to Misprint Division at 0600. Any failure to comply will result in immediate reprinting.')
- Ensure the protagonist's internal reaction reflects the shock of being trapped, not a calm weighing of options.

## Anti-pattern it replaces
Giving the protagonist a clear opt-out or a negotiated choice that undermines the system's authoritarian control and the noir sense of inevitability.
