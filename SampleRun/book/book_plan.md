# The Misprint File

- **Genre:** Dystopian Noir Thriller
- **Tone:** Dystopian noir with bureaucratic absurdity and ticking-clock paranoia.
- **Audience:** Adult

## Premise
A rookie postal carrier discovers her death-date envelope is blank—a clerical anomaly that marks her as both unkillable and a threat to the bureaucratic machine. Drafted by a secret branch of the Fatal Affairs Bureau, she must hunt down others with 'broken dates' while a zealous Auditor tracks her every move to reprint her ending.

## Themes
- Fate vs. free will in a monitored society
- The weaponization of information
- The cost of immortality within a closed system

## Constraints
- No supernatural or fantastical elements; the death predictions are based on an opaque but statistical/corporate algorithm.
- The protagonist's blank envelope makes her functionally immortal but not invulnerable—she can be detained, drugged, or trapped indefinitely.
- All envelope deliveries and Bureau operations follow rigid protocols, even when absurd.
- The story must maintain a sense of bureaucratic dread, avoiding action-hero theatrics.

## World rules
- At birth, every citizen receives a sealed envelope from the Fatal Affairs Bureau containing their predetermined date of death.
- The Bureau’s predictions are never wrong; any deviation is treated as a 'misprint' and must be corrected by the Misprint Division.
- Individuals with broken or blank dates are immune to death by conventional means, making them assets or threats to the system.
- The Bureau monitors all citizens via the postal network, intercepting and analyzing mail to detect anomalies.
- Broken daters can be 'reprinted' by a special process that assigns a new death date, after which they become mortal and can be eliminated.
- The public believes the Bureau provides a service of certainty; questioning it is social heresy.

## Main characters
- Lena Voss — A by-the-book postal carrier who becomes the system's most dangerous glitch.
- Auditor Ambrose Marchetti — A tireless Bureau investigator who sees Lena’s blank envelope as a personal and professional failure.
- Oscar Kole — A charismatic broken-date fugitive who runs an underground railroad for misprints.
- Director Wen — The cold, pragmatic head of the Misprint Division, who drafts Lena into the hunter program.
