# Consolidation report (final)

## Contradictions
- [timeline] ch[2, 3]: The reprint order is issued at 0400, but the Commissioner's office already knows about Lena's contact with Kole (which occurs after 0600 on the same day, after her orientation and field work). This implies the order was issued before the contact occurred.
  fix: Adjust timeline: the contact with Kole occurs the previous day, or the reprint order is issued the next morning after the contact, not at 0400 of the same day.
- [character facts] ch[1, 3]: Lena's blank slip is taken by Internal Affairs officers and sealed in an evidence bag in ch01, but Marchetti presents her original birth slip in ch03, and she also holds her own birth envelope (still sealed) in her pocket. It's unclear how the slip can be both in evidence and in her possession.
  fix: Clarify that the evidence bag contains a certified copy, or that Marchetti retrieved it from evidence to present it, or that Lena has a duplicate blank slip from the feed tray.
- [character facts] ch[2]: Kole claims misprints have seen records of misprints being reprinted after they stop being useful, but Kole himself is a broken-date misprint who has not been reprinted yet. This implies he has seen records that he should not have access to, but no source is established.
  fix: Add a line where Kole says the network has a mole inside the Bureau who leaked records, or that they found documents during a break-in.
- [world rules] ch[1, 2, 3]: The Algorithm's predictions are never wrong, but broken daters exist and are described as having 'corrupted predictions' or 'dates that just broke', which contradicts the infallibility premise.
  fix: Reframe: the Algorithm is statistically infallible for normal citizens, but misprints are a known error rate the Bureau suppresses, or only null outputs are anomalies, while broken dates are externally corrupted (e.g., by the jammer).
- [world rules] ch[2, 3]: Kole says the misprint network has a 'crude jammer', but in ch03, Marchetti states 'locator signatures can be read even when jammed (signal gap reveals silence)'. A crude jammer should not be detectable by the Bureau's postal-intelligence matrix if it creates a complete gap, unless it is intentionally crude and detectable.
  fix: Specify that the jammer is a prototype and the gap it creates is precisely what allowed the Bureau to locate Kole's network, which is why they were rolled up.
