# Table of Contents

## 1. The Blank Envelope
- **Purpose:** Introduce the protagonist Lena Voss, the normalcy of the death-date system, and the shocking discovery of her own blank envelope, which sets the entire plot in motion.
- **Emotional role:** Establish a sense of mundane dread and personal horror as Lena's world is upended by a bureaucratic anomaly.
- **Plot function:** Inciting incident: Lena finds her own envelope is blank, marking her as a misprint and immediately attracting Bureau attention.
- **Setup:** Lena's routine delivery, the public's trust in the Bureau, and the existence of the Misprint Division.
- **Payoff:** Lena's blank envelope is revealed, and she is drafted by Director Wen into the hunter program.

## 2. The Hunter and the Hunted
- **Purpose:** Show Lena's training and first mission as a hunter, while establishing the threat of Auditor Marchetti and the underground resistance led by Oscar Kole.
- **Emotional role:** Shift from confusion to paranoia and moral conflict as Lena is forced to hunt others like her.
- **Plot function:** Rising action: Lena tracks a broken-date fugitive, encounters Oscar Kole's network, and learns the true cost of the Bureau's system.
- **Setup:** Lena's new role, the Bureau's protocol for reprinting, and the existence of a fugitive underground.
- **Payoff:** Lena fails to capture the fugitive, meets Oscar, and begins to question the Bureau's motives.
- **Depends on:** 1

## 3. The Reprint Protocol
- **Purpose:** Force Lena to choose between her own survival and the freedom of other misprints, culminating in a confrontation with Marchetti and a decision that changes the system.
- **Emotional role:** Climactic tension and a bitter, ambiguous resolution that underscores the themes of fate and control.
- **Plot function:** Climax and resolution: Lena is cornered by Marchetti, must decide whether to be reprinted or to help Oscar's network escape, leading to a pyrrhic victory.
- **Setup:** The reprint process, Marchetti's obsession, and Oscar's plan to expose the Bureau.
- **Payoff:** Lena sabotages the reprint machine, allowing some misprints to escape, but Marchetti captures Oscar; Lena becomes a permanent fugitive, her blank envelope still intact.
- **Depends on:** 1, 2
