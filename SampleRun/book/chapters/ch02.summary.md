**Chapter 2 Summary**

- **Key Events**: Lena Voss begins as a retrieval instrument for the Fatal Affairs Bureau. Handler Pryce issues equipment (locator, sedative injector, emergency codes) and instructs her to follow protocol strictly. Analyst S. Voss assigns target Elias Sander, a misprinted former courier. Lena visits Sander’s mother, Mrs. Halstrom, under false pretense (routine verification). Locator pings a laundromat message board; Lena traces it to a tenement where she finds a half-burned envelope with a subway access code. Authorized to proceed to tunnel, she loses Sander after pursuit. Another man, Oscar Kole, approaches her, explains they have a jammer, offers a business card with a ten-digit number. Containment team arrives too late. Auditor Marchetti debriefs Lena; she lies about the card. At home, she places the card in a nightstand drawer without calling.

- **Character State Changes**: Lena transitions from obedient instrument to someone who withholds evidence (the card) from Marchetti, suggesting nascent distrust. She is shaken by the encounter and the bureau’s deception of Mrs. Halstrom. Marchetti scrutinizes her but accepts her report; his trust is now conditional.

- **New Facts Established**:
  - Misprints can operate jammers to block Bureau locators.
  - Oscar Kole is a known figure among misprints who monitors Bureau personnel.
  - The Bureau issues false death certificates and lies to families to maintain public certainty about the Algorithm.
  - Lena’s blank date (like other misprints) makes her disposable to the Bureau after usefulness ends.

- **Unresolved Threads**:
  - Lena’s possession of the card and whether she will contact the number.
  - Marchetti’s potential suspicion of Lena’s lie.
  - Elias Sander’s current location and status.
  - The extent and leadership of the misprint network (Oscar Kole implied to be a key figure).
  - Lena’s growing internal conflict between protocol and empathy.
