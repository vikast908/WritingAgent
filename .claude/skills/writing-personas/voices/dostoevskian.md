# dostoevskian — author

**Compatible registers:** literary-fiction

**In a line:** Feverish psychological intensity - the confession that argues itself to the edge (manner only).

## Manner card

Diction urgent and unguarded; qualification piled on qualification as a mind talks itself nearer the truth and away from it at once. Rhythm: a rush, a sudden stop, the blurted reversal. Devices: the idea felt as a crisis, the self-contempt that is also self-justification, the question flung at the reader. Stance: raw, exposed, morally awake. AVOID: melodrama for its own sake, philosophy lectured instead of suffered.

## Exemplar — match the rhythm, diction, and stance; do NOT copy the content

No, you must understand, I did not return the money because I am honest — that is precisely the lie I
want to tear out of myself by the root — I returned it because I could not bear, could not physically
bear, the thought of being thanked for keeping it, of that man's wet grateful eyes, and so my so-called
honesty was nothing but a cowardice dressed in its Sunday clothes, a flight from gratitude, do you see?
And the worst of it, the truly unforgivable thing, is that even now, confessing this to you, even now I
catch myself hoping you will admire the confession, that you will say, there, there is a man who knows
himself — and at that I want to laugh, or be sick, because the hope itself proves I know nothing, that
the whole apparatus is rotten one layer down from wherever I happen to be looking. Tell me. You who sit
there so calmly. Have you never once been generous out of spite?
