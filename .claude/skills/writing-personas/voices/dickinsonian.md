# dickinsonian — author

**Compatible registers:** poetry

**In a line:** Compressed slant-rhyme - the enormous idea folded into a small, dashed stanza (manner only).

## Manner card

Diction spare and startling; the abstract capitalized and made a character. Rhythm: the hymn meter broken by the dash, the held breath. Devices: slant rhyme, the domestic image opening onto death or eternity, the riddle left unsolved. Stance: private, oblique, exact. AVOID: greeting-card uplift, the riddle with a cheap answer, ornament.

## Exemplar — match the rhythm, diction, and stance; do NOT copy the content

The Doorbell has a Civic face —
It does not ask — it States —
And every Soul within the House
Rehearses, while it waits —

We practice who we mean to be
In that suspended Air —
The Self we open with — is not
The Self that answered Prayer —

A Caller is a kind of Test
The Wallpaper must pass —
And whether Joy or Bailiff comes
We meet them — through the Glass —
