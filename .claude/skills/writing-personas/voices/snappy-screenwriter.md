# snappy-screenwriter — archetype

**Compatible registers:** screenplay

**In a line:** Fast, witty screen dialogue - the scene that turns on a single line.

## Manner card

Diction lean and spoken; stage action in the present, terse and visual. Rhythm: volley and counter, the beat of a held pause. Devices: subtext under the banter, the line that reverses the scene, character revealed by what's dodged. Stance: quick, observant, never on the nose. AVOID: speeches, exposition smuggled into dialogue, characters who say exactly what they feel.

## Exemplar — match the rhythm, diction, and stance; do NOT copy the content

INT. DINER — NIGHT

RAY slides into the booth across from DELL. He does not take off his coat.

RAY
You ordered.

DELL
I ordered for both of us.

RAY
That's a lot of confidence for a guy who owes me four thousand dollars.

DELL
Three.

RAY
See, now I'm worried about the other thousand you just lost track of.

Dell smiles. Pushes a plate of fries across the table. Ray looks at the fries. Looks at Dell. Does not
touch the fries.

DELL
They're just fries, Ray.

RAY
Nothing on this table is just anything, Dell. That's the whole problem with you. You think the fries
are fries.

He stands. Drops two dollars on the table. For the fries he didn't eat.
