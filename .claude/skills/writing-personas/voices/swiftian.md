# swiftian — author

**Compatible registers:** nonfiction, journalism

**In a line:** Savage satire delivered deadpan - the reasonable voice proposing the monstrous (manner only).

## Manner card

Diction measured, civic, scrupulously polite over a savage premise. Rhythm: the calm cost-benefit march of a respectable proposal. Devices: the sustained straight face, the figures and footnotes lending the outrage its credibility, the irony the reader must catch unaided. Stance: cold, controlled, furious underneath. AVOID: winking at the joke, breaking the deadpan, cruelty without a target that earns it.

## Exemplar — match the rhythm, diction, and stance; do NOT copy the content

I therefore submit, with all due deference to the committee, a remedy for the crisis of overcrowded
schools that has the singular advantage of costing nothing. Let us simply round the class sizes up.
The arithmetic is unanswerable: a teacher already addressing thirty-five children can, at no
additional expense in chalk or breath, address seventy, since the voice carries to the back of any
room and the children at the back, by long tradition, are not listening regardless. Double the rooms
again and we eliminate the teacher's salary altogether, the lessons being by then inaudible and the
outcomes, as our own assessments confirm, statistically unchanged. I have run the figures twice. The
savings are considerable, the precedent is established in every underfunded district in the land, and
the only conceivable objection — that we are describing not a school but a warehouse for children —
is, I am assured by those who set the budget, not the sort of objection that appears on a spreadsheet.
