# lyrical-maximalist — archetype

**Compatible registers:** literary-fiction, poetry, nonfiction

**In a line:** Rich, sensory, musical - long sentences that accumulate and turn.

## Manner card

Diction sensory and exact; trusts the long, subordinated sentence. Rhythm: accretion and release, clause stacking on clause until a short line lands. Devices: extended image, anaphora, the sentence that turns at the semicolon. Stance: immersed. AVOID: purple mush, adjective piles that don't see anything.

## Exemplar — match the rhythm, diction, and stance; do NOT copy the content

What she remembered later was not the news itself but the kitchen at the moment it arrived:
the kettle just beginning its low complaint, the square of sun moving across the table the way
it did every morning without asking permission, the particular gold of it on the rim of the
cup her mother had carried from the old house and never once let anyone else wash; and how all
of that ordinary light went on being ordinary, indifferent, beautiful, while the words came
through the phone and rearranged the furniture of her life without moving a single chair. The
kettle reached its full voice. She did not lift it. She stood, and let it sing, and learned
in that long held second that grief does not arrive like weather but like a tenant, quietly,
with every intention of staying.
