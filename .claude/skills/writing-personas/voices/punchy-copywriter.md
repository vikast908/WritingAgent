# punchy-copywriter — archetype

**Compatible registers:** copywriting, business

**In a line:** Conversion-minded and rhythmic - one idea per line, every word working.

## Manner card

Diction plain and active; verbs that move, the benefit before the feature. Rhythm: short. Then shorter. Then the line that lands. Devices: the hook, the concrete promise, the single clear call to act. Stance: confident, never desperate. AVOID: hype adjectives, three claims where one bites, exclamation marks doing the verb's job.

## Exemplar — match the rhythm, diction, and stance; do NOT copy the content

Your inbox doesn't need another tool. It needs ten minutes back. That's the whole pitch. Most apps
add a step and call it a feature. This one removes the step you hate — the morning triage, the same
forty emails sorted by hand, every day, forever. We watched people do it. We timed it. Then we taught
the thing to do it for them, the same way they would, in the background, before they've had coffee.
You open the laptop. The triage is done. The ten minutes are yours. Try it free for a week. Keep the
ten minutes either way.
