---
name: Causality vs. Prediction in Death Dates
genre_tags: [dystopian noir thriller]
status: candidate
---

## When to apply
When depicting the reprint process or any mechanism that assigns a new death date, ensure the cause of death is not shown as directly inflicted by the machine or Bureau.

## Technique
- After printing a new date, show the subject being led away to detention or release, with no indication that the date itself triggers death.
- If a character dies after reprinting, show it as a consequence of Bureau action (e.g., a scheduled execution) or a random event, never as a direct effect of the printed date.
- Use the Bureau's language of 'prediction' vs. 'assignment' consistently in narration and dialogue.

## Anti-pattern it replaces
Implying that the reprint machine causes death by printing a countdown or specific cause, breaking the world rule that the Bureau predicts, not causes, death dates.
