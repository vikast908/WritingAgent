## Canon
- Director Wen is a woman in her fifties with iron-gray hair.
- She offered Lena the choice to join the Misprint Division or be reprinted with a death-date.
- She oversees the correction of Algorithm errors, including misprints.
- Wears a lapel pin of a broken envelope (symbol of Misprint Division).
- Her signature did not appear on the reprint order—only the Commissioner's and the Office of Temporal Compliance's.
- She signed off on Lena's field clearance, but the reprint authorization came from higher up, bypassing her.
