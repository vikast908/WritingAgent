## Canon
- Director Wen offered her a choice: join the Misprint Division as a hunter of other misprints (broken daters), or be assigned a death-date within the year via reprint.
- Lena Voss is a 26-year-old carrier for the Fatal Affairs Bureau, working Route 1103 in the Waterstone district.
- She chose to join the Misprint Division.
- She has been a carrier for eleven weeks.
- She has never opened her own death-date envelope.
- She is now property of the Misprint Division and will report to Processing at 0600 the next day.
- She was labeled a 'misprint' by the Bureau.
- When she retrieved her birth envelope for proactive verification, the slip inside was blank—no date printed.
- Lena accepted the card from Kole and now holds it at her apartment, undecided.
- Lena now wears a gray uniform and the Misprint Division's broken-envelope pin.
- She has been issued a locator, an injector with sedative, and an emergency codes card.
- She has completed orientation and is assigned to an analyst (S. Voss) for her first retrieval operation.
- She lied to Marchetti about Kole's offer, saying 'nothing' was offered.
- She met another misprint, Oscar Kole, who offered her a card with a number.
- She successfully tracked and then lost her target, Elias Sander.
- During the chaos, she bolted for the technician access hatch, climbed down a service corridor and staircase with the thin man and shaved-head woman.
- In the annex, she noticed a safety poster for the Reprint Press Model 4-C and memorized its emergency disengage procedure.
- Instead of initiating the reprint, she snatched a blank slip from the feed tray, causing a jam and triggering emergency maintenance mode.
- She agreed to be taken to the machine.
- She boarded an old subway train on a maintenance spur and pressed the manual launch button.
- She demanded to see the order and the Commissioner's signature, and Marchetti showed it.
- She escaped, leaving Kole behind (he was subdued).
- She now holds her own birth envelope (still sealed) in her pocket.
- She was authorized on the thumb plate.
- She was given a choice: reprint Kole or be reprinted within the hour.
- She was summoned by Internal Affairs officers at her door ten minutes before dawn.
- She was transported to Bureau Corrections Annex – Sector 7.
- She witnessed reprints of two other misprints (a 47-year-old woman, cardiac infarction within 72 hours; a young man, pulmonary failure within one week).
