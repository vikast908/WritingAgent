## Canon
- He left a half-burned envelope in a sink at a tenement on Meridian Row containing a subway access code.
- His file is officially closed with a terminal notation; a death certificate released to next of kin.
- His prediction fractured during a rail-yard accident three years ago; he walked away from a collision that should have crushed his spine.
- Sander is a former Bureau courier, age 34.
- The Bureau issued a recall for reprinting; Sander fled.
