### Chapter 3 Summary — The Reprint Protocol

**Key Events:**
- Lena is taken by two Internal Affairs officers to Bureau Corrections Annex – Sector 7 before dawn.
- En route, she sees a safety poster for the Reprint Press Model 4-C, noting emergency protocols: paper jam or feed failure disengages all restraints and releases pod doors.
- Auditor Ambrose Marchetti presents her original birth slip (blank) and offers a conditional stay: she must personally reprint Oscar Kole, or she will be reprinted within the hour (72-hour terminus).
- Lena demands to see the Commissioner’s order; she memorizes the stamp code from the Office of Temporal Compliance.
- In the reprint chamber, she witnesses two misprints being reprinted: a woman (72 hours, cardiac infarction) and a young man (one week, pulmonary failure). Both are processed and led to detention.
- Kole is chained nearby; he tells Lena the network was rolled up at midnight, but she didn’t talk.
- Lena steps to the thumb plate but instead snatches a blank slip from the feed tray, causing a jam and triggering Emergency Maintenance Mode.
- Restraints on the chair disengage; the holding pod doors open, freeing the thin man and shaved-head woman.
- Kole attacks Marchetti, buying time; Lena escapes with the two misprints through a technician access hatch.
- They descend to a maintenance spur, board an unmarked train, and Lena triggers manual launch.
- Train departs into the tunnel system; Lena sees Marchetti holding her blank slip in its evidence sleeve as the platform recedes.
- Kole is recaptured (baton strike to knees).

**Character State Changes:**
- Lena: from captive/fugitive to active resister; now a fugitive with two misprints, having refused the conditional stay.
- Kole: captured, likely to be reprinted or detained.
- Thin man and shaved-head woman: freed from holding pod, now escaping with Lena.
- Marchetti: frustrated, left on platform; his authority challenged.

**New Facts Established:**
- Locator signatures can be read even when jammed (signal gap reveals silence).
- Commissioner’s office authorized reprint; Director Wen’s signature absent.
- Reprint process: thumb-plate reader, feed tray of blank slips, embossing die prints new date; subject is restrained, then led to detention to live out their terminus.
- Safety protocols: paper jam or feed failure disengages restraints and opens pod doors (Emergency Maintenance Mode).
- Train runs on automatic loop through distribution tunnels; manual launch override exists.

**Unresolved Threads:**
- Kole’s fate (captured; likely to be reprinted or used as leverage).
- Destination of the train; where Lena and the two misprints will emerge.
- Lena’s original birth slip remains in evidence (Marchetti still holds it).
- Marchetti’s pursuit; Bureau response to the escape.
- Director Wen’s absence from the reprint order; her possible knowledge or complicity.
- The stamp code Lena memorized (potential future use).
- Lena’s sealed envelope (blank slip) still in her pocket.
