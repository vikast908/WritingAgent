# Scoring rubric - blind A/B

You're reading two anonymized pieces (**A.md** and **B.md**) on the same prompt. One is Writing
Agent, one is ChatGPT/Claude - you don't know which (the key is hidden until `tally.py`). Judge
**substance, not formatting**; obvious format tells are stripped, but ignore any that slip through.

For each case, in `score_sheet.md`:

1. **Pick a winner** - `A`, `B`, or `tie`. This is the headline metric (win-rate).
2. **Score each side 1–5** on three dimensions (optional but recommended):

| Dimension | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **insight** | makes a specific, contestable argument with non-obvious points | competent but predictable | could appear unchanged on any site |
| **trust / sources** | concrete claims carry credible, specific sources; nothing feels fabricated | some sourcing; a few thin claims | unsourced or smells made-up |
| **readability** | flows, varied rhythm, no AI tells, no re-reads | fine, occasional clunk | generic, em-dash soup, filler |

**How to pick the winner:** which one would you actually publish / send to a smart colleague? Favor a
real argument with evidence over a safe, fluent survey. A tie is allowed but use it sparingly - the
point is a decision.

**Stay honest:** score before you reveal. Don't try to guess which tool wrote which; judge the page.
